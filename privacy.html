Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This page explains which personal data we process when you visit globalvisions.it or write to us, why we do it and what rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Global Visions OÜ, a private limited company registered in the Estonian Business Register with registry code 16697227 (VAT EE102615704), with its registered address at Sepapaja tn 6, 15551 Tallinn, Estonia. You can reach us at hello@globalvisions.it.

What we process and why

  • Emails you send us. If you write to us or ask for a call, we process your name, your email address and whatever you choose to include, only to reply to you and, if you ask, to prepare a proposal. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in answering messages (Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep these emails for as long as the conversation needs, and longer only where the law requires it, for example accounting records if we start working together.
  • Booking a call. Calls are booked through Cal.com, Inc. When you book, you give your name, your email address and any notes you add, which we use only to hold the call. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Cal.com acts as our processor; transfers outside the European Economic Area rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Anonymous, cookieless analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and understand which pages are useful. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. It records aggregated data such as the page visited, the referring site, the browser, the type of device and the country. Visitors are told apart through a hash of the request that is discarded after 24 hours, so we cannot identify you or follow you across days or sites. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the site (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Vercel Inc., which processes technical data such as your IP address and the requested page to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. This data is kept only briefly. Vercel acts as our processor, and transfers outside the European Economic Area rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • Language preference cookie. If you use the EN/IT switcher, we set one technical cookie called lang, with the value en or it, for 12 months. It only remembers the language you chose. It is set only when you click the switcher, and because it is strictly necessary for a feature you asked for, it does not require consent.

What we do not do

No advertising or tracking cookies, no profiling, no sale of data. Fonts and scripts are served from our own domain, so your browser does not contact third-party font or script services when you open the site.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to hello@globalvisions.it and we will answer within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Estonia, the Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, aki.ee), or the authority where you live or work, for example the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali in Italy.

Contact

For anything about privacy: hello@globalvisions.it. If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
