Cookie policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The track store uses very few cookies, and only ones it cannot work without or that you set yourself. That is why there is no cookie banner.

CookieWhat it doesWhen it is setDuration
langRemembers whether you want the site in English or Italian.Only when you use the EN/IT switch.12 months
gv_sessionKeeps you signed in. It holds your Steam ID (or your commercial licence) in a signed form. Strictly necessary. HttpOnly, not readable by scripts.Only when you sign in through Steam or with a licence key.30 days, or until you sign out
gv_stateProtects the Steam sign in against forged requests. Strictly necessary.During sign in through Steam.10 minutes

Stripe

Payment happens on Stripe Checkout, on stripe.com. Stripe uses its own cookies there to process the payment and prevent fraud, under Stripe's cookie policy.

Steam

When you sign in, you go to steamcommunity.com, which uses its own cookies under Valve's policies. We receive only your Steam ID.

No analytics or marketing cookies

We count visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts and scripts are served from our own domain.

Removing cookies

Signing out deletes gv_session. You can delete any cookie from your browser settings at any time. More on how we handle data in the privacy policy.
