05 · Questions

Questions we hear most often.

How do we start?

Book a free 30-minute call. We look at how your company works today and tell you honestly whether and where AI can help.

What does the assessment include?

We map one or more processes with your team, estimate where AI saves time and money, and hand you a written plan with priorities. The price is fixed and agreed before we start.

Do you use off-the-shelf platforms?

No. We write the code ourselves and connect each system to the tools you already use, such as email, WhatsApp, calendars and management software.

Who stays in control of what the AI does?

You do. For anything that matters, like a reply to a guest or a post before it goes live, the system asks a person to approve it.

What happens after launch?

We keep the systems running in production, monitor them and improve them as your processes change.

Where do you work?

We are based in Tallinn, Estonia, and work with companies worldwide, in English and Italian.
